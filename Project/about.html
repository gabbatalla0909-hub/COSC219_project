About

This web application uses Astroworld API to display relevant minecraft materials based on a users given preferences, such as:

  • The Minecraft dimension they are building something in.
  • The category of the biome they are building something in.
  • The rarity of the biome they are building something in.

Materials suggested to the user are thematically based on where they are building, and should purposely complement the atmosphere of the biome.
